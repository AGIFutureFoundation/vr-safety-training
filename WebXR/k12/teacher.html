The load chart is a ratio table

The crane operator's load chart is a ratio table: further out, less load, read before every lift.

  1. Look up at the gantry cranes on the quay.
  2. The further out a load hangs, the bigger its turning effect.
  3. So the chart lets the crane lift less at long reach.

A load moves further out along the boom. What does the chart allow?

  • Less load (answer)
  • The same load

A load further out turns the crane harder, so the chart allows less.
