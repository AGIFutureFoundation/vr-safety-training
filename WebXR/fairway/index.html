Fairway Park

A nine-hole course and an outdoor sports facility, played for real strokes and real scores. Aim, pick a club, time the swing meter, and play the ball as it lies — rough, sand and water all behave differently, and a groundskeeper's log scores how you leave the course behind you.

Grounds crew board

Board done: a grounds station passed, on your passport.

Controls — Keyboard: ←/→ or A/D aim, 1-4 club, Space swing meter (press to start power, again to lock it and start accuracy, again to strike), V camera, C do the groundskeeper courtesy on offer, Esc menu. Touch: the on-screen dial and swing button. Gamepad: left stick aims, A/Cross is the swing-meter button, B/Circle is the courtesy button.

Every hole, club, course feature and mini-game here is original to this platform — no real course, club, brand, league or player is named or modelled.
