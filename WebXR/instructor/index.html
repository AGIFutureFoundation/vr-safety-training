Instructor console

A live view of the training sessions running in other tabs and windows on this machine: where each learner is in the procedure, what they have scored, every unsafe action as it happens, and the verdict when they finish. Select a session to put a line in front of that learner or hold their clock.

Listening…

Selected: no session selected. Nothing on this page is stored — it shows what is happening right now. The durable record of an attempt is the learner's training record, exported from their own app.

This console is same-origin and same-device: it uses a browser BroadcastChannel, which does not cross the network. A hall with a row of laptops, or headsets mirrored to one screen, is covered. An instructor watching from another machine needs the relay, which is not built yet and is not pretended at here. When a simulator is embedded in a learning platform, the same events are already sent to the host page, which is the other honest path. Back to the network portal.
