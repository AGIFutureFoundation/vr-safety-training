Redwood Reach

Four kilometres of coastal redwood and mixed forest on a side: a river valley that winds down to an estuary, fire roads over the ridges, a fire lookout, a sawmill and log yard, a watershed restoration reach, a campground and trail network, a wildland fire station, a nursery and seed bank and a rural substation. Every job board opens a real training station; the crews here work on prevention, preparedness and safe procedure.

Controls — Keyboard: WASD or arrows move and turn, drag the mouse to look, Shift jog, E use a board, a tin or a lesson, R fire-road vehicle, M map, J quests, T advance the clock, Esc close. Touch: the stick moves, drag the view to look, the round buttons act. Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick looks, A uses.

Every place here is original and generic: no real forest, park, company or agency is named or modelled. Limits always read "per the plan, permit or label".
