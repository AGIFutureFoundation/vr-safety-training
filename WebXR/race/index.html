Players on this machine

Each player steers with their own keys, or the gamepad with the same number. Cycle your vehicle with your own left and right keys. The other seats on the grid of eight are AI drivers.

Two-tab race · local multiplayer

Two tabs, one browser

Open this page in a second tab of the same browser. In one tab choose Host, in the other choose Join. The host tab runs the race and the AI; the joining tab sends its controls and draws what the host sends back. The two tabs talk through the browser's BroadcastChannel — nothing leaves this machine, and there is no server.
