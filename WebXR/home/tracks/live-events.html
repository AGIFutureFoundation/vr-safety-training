What it proves

Competency: Rig and power a performance space to entertainment practice

Demonstrated with a mastery run on 3 of its 5 stations, against ETCP Entertainment Technician Certification Program — Certified Rigger; ESTA/ANSI E1.4-1 Manual Counterweight Rigging Systems; NFPA 70E Standard for Electrical Safety in the Workplace.

These stations also count toward:

  • Crane and Rigging — the lift plan, the load and the landing (3 of this track's stations count toward it)

Badges

Each passed level earns an Open Badges 2.0 assertion naming the level, its tasks and their conditions, its lessons and the standards it evidences, with the attempts behind it as evidence, and sends an xAPI statement. Passing level 20, the capstone, climbs the whole track.

A run demonstrates mastery when it earns two or more stars, records zero unsafe actions, answers every interruption it was given, and finishes within 1.5 times the station's par time. One mastery run on enough of a competency's stations earns "demonstrated"; mastery runs on three different days earn "consistent". No other rule earns a competency.

Certification the work leads to: ETCP Certified Rigger and Certified Entertainment Electrician, ANSI E1.4-1 counterweight rigging and NFPA 70E for the power tie-in. No level claims apprenticeship hours: no registry entry states an equivalence.
