What it proves

Competency: Operate and prove a building's boiler, chiller and fire-protection plant

Demonstrated with a mastery run on 3 of its 5 stations, against ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code; ASHRAE Standard 188 Legionellosis: Risk Management for Building Water Systems; NFPA 25 Standard for the Inspection, Testing, and Maintenance of Water-Based Fire Protection Systems.

These stations also count toward:

  • Lockout/Tagout and the control of hazardous energy (1 of this track's stations count toward it)

Badges

Each passed level earns an Open Badges 2.0 assertion naming the level, its tasks and their conditions, its lessons and the standards it evidences, with the attempts behind it as evidence, and sends an xAPI statement. Passing level 20, the capstone, climbs the whole track.

A run demonstrates mastery when it earns two or more stars, records zero unsafe actions, answers every interruption it was given, and finishes within 1.5 times the station's par time. One mastery run on enough of a competency's stations earns "demonstrated"; mastery runs on three different days earn "consistent". No other rule earns a competency.

Certification the work leads to: State stationary engineer licence, ASME boiler practice, ASHRAE 188 water management and NFPA 25 fire-pump testing. No level claims apprenticeship hours: no registry entry states an equivalence.
