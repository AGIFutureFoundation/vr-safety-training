Hotel Workers — Back of House

UNITE HERE hotel housekeepers, laundry and banquet staff

Cal/OSHA's hotel housekeeping musculoskeletal injury prevention standard (8 CCR 3345), the workplace violence prevention plan (8 CCR 3342), 1910.1200 hazard communication for room and laundry chemicals, OSHA 1910.1030 bloodborne pathogens for sharps found in rooms, NFPA 96 where the laundry and kitchen share a plant

The hotel jobs that carry the injuries: turning a room with a housekeeping cart under the state's own hotel ergonomics rule and a panic button on the belt, the laundry plant's chemicals and folder, and a banquet changeover lifted right.

Badges

Each passed level earns an Open Badges 2.0 assertion naming the level, its tasks and their conditions, its lessons and the standards it evidences, with the attempts behind it as evidence, and sends an xAPI statement. Passing level 20, the capstone, climbs the whole track.

A run demonstrates mastery when it earns two or more stars, records zero unsafe actions, answers every interruption it was given, and finishes within 1.5 times the station's par time. One mastery run on enough of a competency's stations earns "demonstrated"; mastery runs on three different days earn "consistent". No other rule earns a competency.

Certification the work leads to: Cal/OSHA's hotel housekeeping musculoskeletal injury prevention standard (8 CCR 3345), the workplace violence prevention plan (8 CCR 3342), 1910.1200 hazard communication for room and laundry chemicals, OSHA 1910.1030 bloodborne pathogens for sharps found in rooms, NFPA 96 where the laundry and kitchen share a plant. No level claims apprenticeship hours: no registry entry states an equivalence.
