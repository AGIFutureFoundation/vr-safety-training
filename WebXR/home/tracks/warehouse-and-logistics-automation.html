Warehouse and Logistics Automation — Teamsters Distribution Floor

Teamsters (IBT) warehouse and logistics automation training

Teamsters (IBT) warehouse and logistics automation training, tested against OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147, 1910.178, 1910.212, 1910.132, 1910.1200, 1910.28 and 1910.36, 29 CFR 1926.602, ASME B20.1, ANSI R15.06 and ISO 10218 for robots, ASHRAE 15 and ANSI/IIAR 2 and 6 for refrigeration, and the Revised NIOSH Lifting Equation across eight distinct union jobs

Eight jobs on a modern automated distribution floor: an AMR traffic zone entered and locked out the way the fleet's own controller expects it, a conveyor jam cleared behind a proven isolation, a high-bay order picker ridden with a harness clipped to its own anchor, a trailer proven restrained before a forklift ever crosses the leveler, a lift-truck battery changed in a bay whose eyewash and ventilation were actually confirmed, a robotic palletizer cell entered on a permit and proven at zero energy, a cold room worked with a buddy actually checked in, and a pick-to-light module run at a pace and a posture that hold up for a whole shift. Every station ends on a proof — a gauge, a locked-and-tagged isolation, a signed log — rather than on how routine the task felt going in.

What it proves

Competency: Enter, isolate and prove eight distinct Teamsters warehouse and logistics automation jobs

Demonstrated with a mastery run on 4 of its 8 stations, against OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147 The control of hazardous energy (lockout/tagout); OSHA 29 CFR 1910.178 Powered industrial trucks; OSHA 29 CFR 1910.212 General requirements for all machines; ANSI/ASSP R15.06 Safety requirements for industrial robots and robot systems; ISO 10218 Robots and robotic devices — safety requirements for industrial robots; ASHRAE 15 Safety Standard for Refrigeration Systems.

Badges

Each passed level earns an Open Badges 2.0 assertion naming the level, its tasks and their conditions, its lessons and the standards it evidences, with the attempts behind it as evidence, and sends an xAPI statement. Passing level 20, the capstone, climbs the whole track.

A run demonstrates mastery when it earns two or more stars, records zero unsafe actions, answers every interruption it was given, and finishes within 1.5 times the station's par time. One mastery run on enough of a competency's stations earns "demonstrated"; mastery runs on three different days earn "consistent". No other rule earns a competency.

Certification the work leads to: Teamsters (IBT) warehouse and logistics automation training, tested against OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147, 1910.178, 1910.212, 1910.132, 1910.1200, 1910.28 and 1910.36, 29 CFR 1926.602, ASME B20.1, ANSI R15.06 and ISO 10218 for robots, ASHRAE 15 and ANSI/IIAR 2 and 6 for refrigeration, and the Revised NIOSH Lifting Equation across eight distinct union jobs. No level claims apprenticeship hours: no registry entry states an equivalence.
