Cohort guides

Run sheets a teacher or a union hall can follow to run Louisiana Development Training Programme with a room of people: what to prepare, who does what, each session in order with timed blocks that open real stations, simulations, lesson flows and games, then the debrief, the assessment and the close-out. 31 guides: a K-12 classroom guide for the six Louisiana lessons, and a run sheet for each of the 30 DEAN templates (6 pathways × 5 levels).

The Louisiana Development Training Programme is the platform's own training programme. It has no partnership with SpaceX, Meta, Applied Digital, Shintech, Black Bayou, Saronic, AVEX, Woodside, Louisiana Economic Development or any union, and it does not deliver or describe any employer's or union's programme or hiring. Company names identify the public projects the sources describe; union names are trade references from the platform's registry; every practice is taught from each station's own cited safety standards. Project site layouts on the maps are the platform's illustration: the parishes, waterways and towns are real.

Minutes are this guide's planning allowances (a station about 20, a simulation about 30, an opening 10 and a debrief 15; a session is split before 90), not facts about any place or programme. Every practice is taught from each station's own cited safety standards.

K-12 classroom

Six lessons, one per session: look at the place, run the station, answer one check question, play a two-minute game, close on the learner's own words.

K-12 Louisiana lessons — classroom guide 6 sessions · module mod-lco-la-k12 · score 60 · due in 14 days

For: upper primary and lower secondary classes. Run by: a teacher or a school district, or a union hall's school outreach.

Where the work is on the maps: New Marsh from Mud: la-starbase-vermilion/lsb-marsh-creation-dredge; How a Lock Lifts a Boat: nola-bywater-lower-ninth/nbw-canal-lock-crew; Follow the Energy: la-meta-richland/lmr-substation-build; What Lifts a Wing: la-avex-new-iberia/lav-apron-work; Why Steel Can Float: la-saronic-franklin/lsf-new-slip-build; Who Builds It: la-shintech-plaquemine/lsp-workforce-centre

Roles

  • Lead (teacher or outreach instructor) — Opens each session, runs the lessons and stations, reads the check questions aloud and leads the debrief.
  • Second adult — Watches the headset space, helps anyone stuck on a device and keeps the room calm and on time.

Before the first session

  • Set up the class: an organisation, a cohort with a seat per learner and its class code, with the module mod-lco-la-k12 assigned (lcoSetUpClassroom, or the instructor console).
  • Check every device on the class code; a browser on a desktop, tablet or phone is enough, and headsets need clear space around each learner.
  • Play each lesson's flow once yourself: the pre-brief, the station, the check question and the two-minute game.
  • Keep to the classroom rule: plain words, one idea at a time, no fear framing. The lessons teach general science and name kinds of work and the apprenticeship route, never any one employer's jobs.
