Bay Restoration Academy

One programme an employer, a union hall, a public agency or a school district can run for the restoration work the U.S. EPA's San Francisco Bay Program awards and the Port of Oakland's Clean Ports program describe. Each project track traces the work the sources state, to the crafts that do it, to the stations, simulations and K-12 lessons that teach it, to a credential on the competency layer.

See the project tracks Run a cohort

The Bay Restoration Academy is the platform's own training programme. It has no partnership with any grant recipient, workforce organisation or union, and it does not deliver or describe their programmes. Union names are trade references from the platform's registry; every practice is taught from the station's own cited safety standards.

The work types are quoted from the sources: U.S. EPA — EPA Awards Record $82 Million to Improve Water Quality and Restore Habitat Across San Francisco Bay (22 September 2026); Stormwater (trade press) — coverage of the awards; Port of Oakland — Clean Ports; Port of Oakland — awarded historic $322 million EPA grant. The sources name eight of the twenty projects; the other twelve are not named, placed or trained for here.

How the Academy runs

On the layers the platform already has — nothing new to install, no payment in the public build.

  1. 1 · Organisation and seats
    A coordinator creates the organisation and a cohort with its seat count in the instructor console (org layer). Seat licensing is a seat count; the public build takes no payment.
  2. 2 · Class code
    Each cohort gets an invite code; learners join with it and choose whether to share progress.
  3. 3 · Module
    Load the track's DEAN template for the role, set the due date and the required score, assign it to the class code.
  4. 4 · Train
    Stations in SmartCiti.X, full-procedure simulations on the walkable maps, K-12 lessons in class. Assigned lessons glow on the site boards.
  5. 5 · Debrief and assess
    The instructor guide for each module: objectives, the station's cited practice, debrief prompts, the assessment rule (handbook).
  6. 6 · Credential
    Mastery runs demonstrate the pathway's competency: Open Badges, a transcript and an xAPI export from the competency layer, and the cohort certificate.

Credential ladder

Every pathway ends in an existing competency (13 across the Academy); where the track's own stations do not yet meet its mastery rule, the module adds a capstone from the competency's list.

  • Awareness and K-12 (school districts, public-agency staff and community members; module score 60, due 14 days after the cohort starts) → k12-science Observe, test fairly and explain, keeping what was seen apart from what was hoped and clearer apart from safe; k12-practical-math Use measurement, proportion, data and chance to reason about real places, with every unit written and every answer checked
  • Pre-apprentice / entry (pre-apprenticeship cohorts and new hires; module score 70, due 21 days after the cohort starts) → core-trenching Trenching and Excavation — protective systems and the competent person; core-confined-space Confined Space entry, attendance and atmosphere; core-respiratory-protection Respiratory Protection — selection, proving and working on air; wojrc-pathway-edition Walk the pathway: intake to a signed enrolment, a first paycheck and a matched alumni mentor
  • Apprentice (apprentices in the crafts the work involves; module score 80, due 42 days after the cohort starts) → bay-restoration-maritime-underwater Work the water side of a Bay restoration and clean-up job: dive, vessel, sediment, shoreline and monitoring crews; bay-program-projects Clean out a trash capture device, dig a rain garden, work a tidal channel from mats, feed chemicals at a treatment plant, sample a PCB hotspot with chain of custody and keep a fish passage working, each by its own safe procedure; heavy-equipment-operators Run the machine, prove the assembly and hold the crew's own controls before the load moves; hazmat-environmental Contain, decontaminate and monitor a hazardous-materials release; energy-transition Commission and isolate photovoltaic, storage and charging systems
  • Journeyworker refresher (journey-level workers taking an annual or pre-job refresher; module score 85, due 14 days after the cohort starts) → core-trenching Trenching and Excavation — protective systems and the competent person; core-confined-space Confined Space entry, attendance and atmosphere; hazmat-environmental Contain, decontaminate and monitor a hazardous-materials release; core-lockout-tagout Lockout/Tagout and the control of hazardous energy
  • Supervisor / crew lead (forepersons, crew leads and agency inspectors; module score 90, due 28 days after the cohort starts) → bay-program-projects Clean out a trash capture device, dig a rain garden, work a tidal channel from mats, feed chemicals at a treatment plant, sample a PCB hotspot with chain of custody and keep a fish passage working, each by its own safe procedure; situational-awareness Hold the procedure while the site interrupts you; hazmat-environmental Contain, decontaminate and monitor a hazardous-materials release; energy-transition Commission and isolate photovoltaic, storage and charging systems
