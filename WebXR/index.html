Safety Campus

Six workplace bays, each with four conditions: two real hazards and two controlled look-alikes. Nothing is marked before you commit. Correct hazard +100, false positive −25 once, repeats score nothing — the same deterministic rules as the headset build.

Desktop: W A S D move · drag to look · click to inspect.
Headset: open this page in the Meta Quest Browser, then Enter VR — point and pull the trigger; left stick glides, right stick snap-turns.

Immersive mode needs a WebXR browser (Meta Quest Browser, or a PC headset via a WebXR-capable desktop browser). Ray-Ban Meta display glasses cannot run immersive WebXR — on those, this page is view-only through the phone. Scoring runs entirely in this page; nothing is transmitted.
