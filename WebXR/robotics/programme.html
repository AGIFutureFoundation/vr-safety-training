Holodeck Robotics & Human–Robot Collaboration Programme

One programme an employer, a training centre, a union hall or a school can run for people who work beside robots: industrial cells, cobots, AMRs, construction robots, robot maintenance and the data and AI-training roles. Five levels — awareness / K-12, robot operator, technician, integrator / safety lead and AI-training specialist — each made of scored stations and ending in a credential on the competency layer.

See the tracks The learning loop Robot arena Run a cohort

The Holodeck Robotics & Human–Robot Collaboration Programme is the platform's own training programme. It has no partnership with any robot maker, integrator, software platform, investor or union, and it does not deliver or describe any employer's or union's programme. Robot cells, cobots, AMRs and construction robots in the stations are generic and procedural. Standards are named so learners can find them; their text is not quoted, and each station teaches the practice in general terms from its own cited sources.

Robot stations at every level

A level counts as covered when at least one of its own stations (capstones not counted) puts a robot at the centre of the practice; an AI-training level also needs a station from the learning loop. 30 of 30 track levels are covered in this build.

The learning loop at the AI-training level

People demonstrate, a robot policy learns from the demonstrations, the policy is evaluated on seeds it never saw, and the robot demonstrates back. Each mechanism is named for what it is.

  • Consent first — dx-data.js dxOptIn(); taught at Collecting a Robot Demonstration Safely robot
    An adult, signed-in learner opts in on DATAWORKS' consent screen; K-12, demo and signed-out sessions never collect. The data stays on the device and revoking deletes it.
  • Demonstrate — col-learn.js colDemosFromEpisodes(); taught at Collecting a Robot Demonstration Safely robot
    Consented teleoperation episodes become demonstrations; an episode without a consent receipt is refused. Tests use synthetic demonstrations from the scripted expert with noise (colSyntheticDemos), labelled synthetic.
  • Train a policy (behaviour cloning) — col-learn.js colTrain(); taught at Reviewing a Robot Policy's Evaluation robot
    A k-nearest-neighbour behaviour-cloning policy copies what the demonstrations did in similar moments. It does not plan and does not generalise beyond its demonstrations.
  • Evaluate on held-out seeds — col-learn.js colEvalScenario(); taught at Reviewing a Robot Policy's Evaluation robot
    The policy runs on seeds it never saw, against the scripted expert and a random baseline; the success rate and the gap to the expert are reported as measured.
  • The robot demonstrates back — col-learn.js colGhost(); taught at Reviewing a Robot Policy's Evaluation robot
    The trained policy replays a task as a ghost, with a plain-language explanation of each choice from its features (colExplain, a heuristic, no language model), so the learner can watch it and then try it.

Recorder — rt-teleop.js rtRecorder(); taught at Collecting a Robot Demonstration Safely robot. A controller (or mouse) pose drives the Teleop Pick-and-Place arm; each take is recorded as a demonstration through DATAWORKS' recorder, so nothing is kept unless an adult, signed-in learner has opted in, and revoking deletes it. Tests use a scripted human stand-in, labelled synthetic.

Gap stations (ROBOTRAIN): 4 of 4 covered — safe teach-pendant use (enabling device, reduced speed): Teaching a Robot from the Pendant robot; cobot power-and-force-limiting check (ISO/TS 15066 named): Measuring a Cobot's Contact Forces robot; recovering a robot after an e-stop: Bringing a Robot Back After an E-stop robot; speed-and-separation monitoring set-up: Setting Up Speed-and-Separation Monitoring robot.

Training data is collected only with opt-in consent from signed-in adults — never in K-12, demo or signed-out sessions. It stays on the device (there is no upload endpoint), and revoking consent deletes it and marks dependent policies stale.
