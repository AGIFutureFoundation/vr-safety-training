Bay World · on the water

Bay Regatta

Twelve motor yachts berthed around the bay, a calendar of hosted events, and three race courses. Every cast-off starts with the briefing — the guest count read back, a life jacket for every person aboard plus a spare, the muster point named — and every run is scored the way a skipper is judged: each mark on its side, no-wake speed through the harbour mouth, the give-way vessel keeping clear at a crossing, and a clean docking home. Reputation and credits go into the same career ledger as Bay World.

Racing is inside: enter the harbour, then choose Race the fleet.

Right of way here is taught the way a skipper learns it — when another vessel crosses from your starboard side you are the give-way vessel and keep clear by easing down or turning away. Every yacht, course and event is original to this platform; the score is a plain result and nothing is bought or staked.
