# Homepage and Guide brief — a front door that draws people in, and a Guide that knows the whole platform

Binds MARQUEE (console MARQUEE: the homepage) and COMPASS (console COMPASS: the Guide). `console-brief.md`, `WebXR/ACCESSIBILITY.md` and the shared control grammar in `WebXR/shared/controls.js` apply. The homepage is generated by `tools/gen_home.mjs` into `WebXR/index.html` and `WebXR/home.html` (and the dist copy by the bundler); edit the generator, never the output.

## MARQUEE — the homepage
- A vivid hero: a live, lightweight animated scene (canvas or a small three.js scene using the shared builders at low detail — a Bay World skyline at dusk, a yacht crossing, a diver's light below the surface) behind one headline and two clear actions ("Start playing" into Bay World, "Find your trade" into the programme finder). Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` (a still frame instead) and loads nothing from the network beyond what the pages already load.
- World cards with real in-game captures: generate small JPEG thumbnails of Bay World, the Regatta, the Deep, Fairway Park, the Atlas, SmartCiti.X and the Holodeck headlessly (Playwright at `/opt/node22/lib/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs`, executable `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium`, pages served from `WebXR/dist/` with the vendored three.js as `tools/check_mobile.mjs` does) into `WebXR/home/img/` at ≤ 60 KB each, and inline them into the generated page as data URIs (the published build caps its file count).
- A programme finder: search and filter by trade, union, category and world, each result a card with its colour, its union line, station count, the learner's progress chip (`ppProgressChip` from `WebXR/shared/passport.js`) and one "Start" action.
- A "Continue where you left off" strip from the passport, empty-state friendly for a first visit.
- Friendly structure: a short "How it works" strip (play → take a job → pass the procedure → earn the credential), the unions strip, then the full catalogue below the fold. Mobile first: one column at 360 px, thumb-reachable actions, text ≥ 16 px.
- `tools/check_home.mjs` (extend): the hero, the two actions, the finder, the world cards with images, the continue strip, reduced-motion handling, no layout overlap at 360 and 1280 widths.

## COMPASS — the Guide
- `WebXR/shared/guide.js` (prefix `gd…`): a floating "Guide" button on every page (home, every app bundle, the track pages), draggable by mouse and touch, snapping to the nearest edge, position remembered in localStorage (try/catch), keyboard reachable, never covering the touch stick or primary HUD (read `touch.js` and the HUD layouts; `check_ui.mjs` overlap rules apply).
- Opens a panel with text chat and optional voice: speech out through `WebXR/shared/voice-assist.js`, speech in through the Web Speech API's recognition where the browser has it (a mic button that hides when unsupported). The Guide's tone follows `ei-guide.js`: plain, kind, never blaming.
- Knowledge: `tools/gen_guide_kb.mjs` builds `WebXR/shared/guide-kb.js` from the whole app — every programme and station (title, tagline, union, category, first `why` line), every world and its sites and activities, the controls table, the passport's features, the Atlas, the docs pages' headings and first paragraphs (`docs/*.md`, `docs/programmes/*.md`), and a FAQ it writes from those sources (how to start, how progress is saved, how to find my union, what works on a phone, what a star means). Answers come from an on-device retrieval index (BM25 or TF-IDF over the chunks, no network): the top chunks are composed into a short answer with links that navigate ("Open the Pathway Edition", "Take me to the pier pilings"). Size-capped (≤ 600 KB) and loaded lazily when the panel first opens.
- An optional hosted-model hook: `guideConfig.endpoint` in `WebXR/auth-config.json` (null by default); when set, the Guide sends the question plus its retrieved chunks there and shows the reply, otherwise it answers on-device. No key is ever in the repo.
- Facts rule: the Guide answers only from the knowledge base; when nothing matches it says so and offers the finder. About wojrc.org it may say only the sourced text in `tools/briefs/wojrc-brief.md`.
- `tools/check_guide.mjs` in `check_all`: the KB is fresh (re-run and diff), 20 scripted questions each return an answer whose links resolve to real pages or stations, the button is on every page, draggable, persists its position, opens with keyboard, the mic hides without support, the endpoint is null, no key strings.

## Process (both)
Start with `git fetch origin claude/vr-ar-safety-training-wkwmve && git merge --ff-only FETCH_HEAD`. Keep your console file. The bundler concatenates every module into one scope and erases import aliases: prefix new top-level names. Commit in steps; each commit message ends with the two trailer lines in your task. Gate with `python3 tools/bundle_webxr.py` and `node tools/check_all.mjs` (exact "All N checkers pass" line). Hand back within 40 minutes: ≤200 words, commit hashes, the check_all line.
