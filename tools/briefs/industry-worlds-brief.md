# Industry worlds brief — wind farm, data centres, aerospace and defence depots, robotics factories, each with simulation options

Binds TURBINE (console TURBINE: wind farm, data centre build, ocean data centre) and ORBIT (console ORBIT: aerospace and defence depot training, robotics factory and training centre). `station-brief.md`, `assets-brief.md`, `wave100-brief.md`, `console-brief.md` and the facts rule apply: generic, original places; no real company, agency site, programme, mission, vehicle or person; no numbers invented for loads, voltages, pressures, heights, depths or limits ("per the manufacturer's manual", "per the site's procedure", "per the work order").

## What "simulation options" means here
Every new district (registered in `WebXR/smartcity/js/districts.js` under the scenic budget, low vignette) and every new station supports the platform's options: `?weather=` (wind for the turbine and the ocean pod matters to the procedure), `?time=`, the interruption drill (`?interrupts=`), and a new `?fault=` option the station declares (for example `yaw-brake-fault`, `crah-alarm`, `leak-detect`, `estop-trip`) that changes the scene and one step's correct answer. Document it in `docs/districts.md` and assert it in the checker.

- Eight stations: turbine climb and rescue-kit check, nacelle lockout and yaw-brake fault, blade inspection from a platform (weather decides the go/no-go), substation switching under a permit, data-hall busway install and torque sign-off, raised-floor tile lift and cable tray safety, CRAH alarm response in a live hall, ocean pod retrieval and hatch opening on a workboat (diving limits "per the dive plan").

## ORBIT (prefix `ad-` stations, programme `aerospace-defense-and-robotics`, categories "Manufacturing & Automation" / "Mobility & Transit"; unions: `iam`, `afge`, `ifpte`, `uaw` and their training bodies as the registry lists them; add a union only with a null-file brand entry and a `source: "unverified"` training body)
- Scope is civilian-style safety training at the level a federal aerospace or depot workforce trains: ESD and cleanroom discipline, a payload crane lift with a lift plan, hazardous-fluid servicing with PPE and a buddy (generic: "the fluid named on the work order"), aircraft depot maintenance with tool control and foreign-object checks, a propulsion test stand's exclusion zone and countdown holds. - Eight to ten stations across both, then the programme and competency.
