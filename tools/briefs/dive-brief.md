# Dive and ecology brief — commercial diving, scientific scuba, and marine ecology packs

Binds DIVE1 (console TENDER, prefix `cd-`, programme `commercial-diving-and-scientific-scuba`) and ECO1 (console KELP, prefix `me-`, programme `marine-ecology-and-restoration`). `station-brief.md`, `ladder-brief.md`, `assets-brief.md`, `proof-brief.md`, `wave100-brief.md`, `console-brief.md` and `bayarea-brief.md`'s facts rule apply. Depth, gas, decompression, current, temperature and time limits are never stated: "per the dive plan and the tables the supervisor holds". No species fact, count, date or place history is asserted; ecology content teaches the method, not a claim about the bay.

Existing stations to read for style and to avoid repeating: `br-dive-site-hazard-assessment-and-jsa`, `br-dive-tender-and-umbilical-management`, `br-surface-supplied-dive-station-setup`, `br-derelict-gear-recovery-dive`, `br-underwater-debris-survey-and-mapping`, `br-underwater-sediment-core-sampling`, `mw-dive-supervisor-and-dive-plan`, `mw-diver-emergency-and-recovery`, `mw-hull-inspection-and-cleaning-dive`, `eelgrass-transplant`.
