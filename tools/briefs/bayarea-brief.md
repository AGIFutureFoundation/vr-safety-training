# Bay Area Union Edition brief — sheet metal, bridge, port maintenance, marine and water

Binds every task that builds this edition; `station-brief.md`, `ladder-brief.md`, `proof-brief.md` and `loop-brief.md` apply.

## The edition
Four packs in one programme, `bay-area-union-edition`, each a ten-level ladder once the ladder layer lands:
- **Sheet metal** (SMART and its International Training Institute): shop fabrication, duct, architectural and industrial sheet metal, welding and testing, air balancing, service.
- **Bridge** (Ironworkers and IMPACT, IUPAT/FTI bridge painters, the Pile Drivers of the Carpenters): the Golden Gate Bridge as the working scene: tower, main cable, suspender ropes, deck, the traveller, the paint programme, the fog.
- **Port maintenance** (ILWU and the PMA training programme, IUOE stationary and crane maintenance): crane and straddle-carrier maintenance, spreader and twist-lock inspection, reefer power, dock fenders, bollards, terminal lighting, storm-water at the terminal.
- **Marine and water** (the Inlandboatmen's Union of the ILWU, MEBA, SIU, the Pile Drivers' commercial divers): ferry and workboat operations under USCG Subchapter M, mooring and towing, oil transfer watch, and **underwater work**: pier pile inspection, hull inspection and cleaning, underwater welding and cutting, dive supervision under OSHA 29 CFR 1910 Subpart T and ADCI consensus standards.

## Scenes
Two new stage districts, built once by the scene team and used by the packs: `golden-gate-deck` (a suspension-bridge deck and tower in International Orange with main cable, suspender ropes, a traveller, a lane closure, the bay and fog; the horizon opens to the strait) and `bay-underwater` (a submerged scene: blue-green fog, a caustic light pattern, bubbles, a pier's piles with marine growth, a hull side, a dive stage, an umbilical to the surface; the HUD adds depth and bottom time). Stations set `district: "golden-gate-deck"` or `district: "bay-underwater"`; the scene team ships a checker that the districts build, stay inside budget and are reachable, and a `?district=` preview.

## Facts and names
- The Golden Gate Bridge opened in 1937 and is painted International Orange; state nothing else about its history unless the repo sources it. Do not name individual workers, past or present.
- Name a union local only when certain; otherwise the international and its training body as the registry lists them (`smart-iti`, `ironworkers-impact`, `iupat-fti`, `carpenters-ictf`, `ilwu-pma-training`, `meba-calhoon-school`, `siu-paul-hall-center`, `uscg-subchapter-m`). Add registry entries for OSHA 1910 Subpart T, ADCI, AWS D3.6 underwater welding, ANSI/ASSP A10 bridge painting containment (SSPC-QP) and NAVFAC or USCG diving references only with the body and title and `source: "unverified"` unless the citation form is certain.
- Depth, gas, decompression and current limits are stated only as "per the dive plan and the tables the supervisor holds", never as numbers the station invents.

## Content rules
Every station follows the station brief in full (12–15 steps, ≥6 kinds incl. drag/turn/gauge/track, 4 hazards, 2 interruptions, `why` ≥200 chars, ≥5 registry-form authorities, `supportLine`, crew check-in, closing log; eval ≥95, `std` 100). Level 7+ chains in the pack declare an instructor-injectable interruption. Underwater stations use `track` for bottom time or gas, `gauge` for depth or pneumo, `hold` for the umbilical check, `drag` for tool passing on the stage.
