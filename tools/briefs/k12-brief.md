# K-12 brief — practical maths, science, history and more, taught inside the SmartCiti.X worlds, with Cognition.X flows

Binds SCHOLAR (console SCHOLAR). `station-brief.md` (the interaction, hazard and interruption bar still applies, with "hazards" read as misconceptions and unsafe shortcuts), `ladder-brief.md`, `console-brief.md` and the facts rule apply.

## Review first
Read `WebXR/shared/flowhub.js`, `docs/flowhub.md` and `WebXR/flows/*.json`: FlowHub is the SmartCiti.X side of a host-orchestrated flow; the repository holds **no Cognition.X or FlowHub specification**, and nothing may be written as knowledge of Cognition.X's internals. Write `docs/k12.md` with a short review of what the flow contract offers a school (ordered nodes, conditions on edges, check-ins, hand-back to the host) and how the K-12 programmes use it, labelled as the SmartCiti.X side only.

## Build
1. **Standards, honestly.** Add registry entries (in `tools/standards.json`, `source: "unverified"`, generic titles, no clause numbers) for: the UN Sustainable Development Goal 4 (Quality Education) as a framework, UNESCO's education guidance as a body, the INEE Minimum Standards for Education in emergencies as a body, and generic "national curriculum framework" as a category. K-12 stations cite these only as frameworks the lesson is aligned to, never as certifying bodies. No invented grade-level codes.
2. **Programmes (ages and bands named generically: early primary, upper primary, lower secondary, upper secondary):**
   - `k12-practical-math` — eight stations set in the worlds: measuring and scaling the basketball court (area, perimeter, ratio), a household budget with a first paycheck (percentages), reading a map scale in Bay World (distance, speed, time), fractions in the kitchen station set, slope and angles on a ramp at a construction site, graphing tide data at the pier (no invented tide values: the learner plots readings the scene shows), probability with a fair spinner at the arcade, geometry of a wind turbine's blade sweep.
   - `k12-science` — eight stations: the water cycle and filtration at a treatment plant, buoyancy and pressure in the Deep (qualitative, no depth figures), energy transfer at the wind farm, circuits at an electrical bench (safe low-voltage framing), ecosystems at the kelp transect, weather and the sky layer, simple machines at a crane, the scientific method with a controlled experiment.
   - `k12-history-and-civics` — six stations that teach **historical method**, not contested claims: reading a primary source against a secondary source, building a timeline from dated documents in the scene (dates shown in the scene are the lesson's own fictional archive, labelled as such), oral history interview skills, how a local council meeting works (generic), the history of work and trade guilds told only as widely established general facts or framed as "research this" prompts, map literacy across eras (fictional maps).
   - `k12-literacy-and-life-skills` — six stations: reading instructions and safety labels, writing a clear incident report, public speaking at a hall, digital citizenship and online safety, first aid awareness (call for help, never a clinical step), teamwork and feedback (link to the EI stations).
   Each station is anchored at a Bay World, Deep or district site and launched from a job board; each programme has a competency and a ladder.
3. **Flows:** four FlowHub flows in `WebXR/flows/` (one per programme) with brief nodes, the stations, check-ins and a teacher gate; validated by `tools/check_flowhub.mjs`.
4. **A school view:** a "Classroom" entry on the homepage finder (a filter for the K-12 programmes) and a teacher note in each programme overview on running it in a low-connectivity setting (the app works offline once loaded; phones at low quality).
5. `tools/check_k12.mjs` in `check_all`: every K-12 station meets the bar, every citation resolves, no grade codes or invented figures, every programme has a flow that validates, and every station is launchable from a world.
