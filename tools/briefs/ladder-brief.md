# Ladder brief — twenty levels, seventy-five lessons, per programme

Every programme in `WebXR/smartcity/js/curricula.js` is a twenty-level training track. This brief binds every task that builds ladder infrastructure or ladder content; `station-brief.md`, `proof-brief.md` and `loop-brief.md` still apply.

## Lessons, tasks, conditions
- A **lesson** is one station step run under one **condition**. A **task** is `{ station, condition }`. A level's lesson total is the sum of its tasks' step counts, read headless from the real station modules; nobody types it.
- The conditions (defined once in `WebXR/shared/ladder.js`, each launched by a query parameter SmartCiti.X reads): `base` (as authored); `time:day`, `time:dusk` (`?time=`; night is the stage default and so is the base run); `weather:<kind>` (`?weather=`, a `shared/weather.js` kind, outdoor stations only); `hazard` (`?hazard=assess`, hazard mode pinned to assessed); `interrupt:<id>` (`?interrupt=`, one declared interruption fired off its authored step); `variant:assessment`, `variant:pressure` (`?variant=&seed=`, `shared/variants.js`). There is no dawn in `stage.js` and no heat in `weather.js`; neither is offered until the rendering layer builds it. Trade Skills reads none of these, so a Trade Skills room is always `base`.
- A condition is only used where the station honestly changes under it (`conditionValid`): no weather indoors, no interruption the station does not declare, no hazard mode without a registered hazard.
- Rules every level keeps: no pair twice in a level, no pair shared with the level above or below. The generator also runs each station at most once per level: the same procedure twice back to back under a lighting change would count its steps twice without teaching them twice.

## The ladder
- A **level** is an ordered chain of tasks run back to back with one shared score, one results card and one badge. It holds **at least 75 lessons**; a level with fewer is `partial: true` with its shortfall and is counted in the content gap table in `docs/ladders.md`, never hidden and never padded.
- Bands: 1–5 orientation and single procedures (base, then daylight and dusk); 6–10 the same under weather and the hour; 11–14 under hazard mode and injected interruptions; 15–18 multi-station chains (at least two tasks, at least one declared interruption, one task run under it, so `CMD_INTERRUPT` has something to fire); 19 a full shift (at least five tasks); 20 the capstone: the hardest stations as assessment variants, under the mastery rule with no coaching. Level gates use `shared/competency.js`'s mastery rule; nothing else unlocks a level.
- The ladder is **data** (`WebXR/smartcity/js/ladders.js`, generated by `tools/gen_ladders.mjs` from the programme's stations sorted by difficulty, with hand-tuned entries kept in `ladders.overrides.json`; an override task is `"app:id@condition"`). Checkers gate: twenty levels per programme, every task's station exists, every condition valid for its station, the pair rules, lesson totals computed from the real modules, the bands, the capstone at level 20.
- The **content gap** is simulated, not divided: hypothetical 13-step stations are added to a programme one at a time and the ladder regenerated until every level reaches 75. That number is the content a programme needs; the stations themselves are written by content teams under the station brief.
- **Union standards** live in `tools/standards.json`; a level names the standards its tasks evidence (union of its stations' registry hits) and the apprenticeship-hour equivalence only where the registry entry states one. Never invent hours, clauses or a body's endorsement.

## Track pages
- `tools/gen_tracks.mjs` (run by `gen_catalog.mjs`) writes one page per programme, `WebXR/home/tracks/<id>.html`, bundled to `WebXR/dist/tracks/<id>.html`: the ladder with lessons and conditions per level, station cards with a screenshot thumbnail where `docs/screenshots/**/<id>*.png` exists (`node tools/gen_tracks.mjs --thumbs` refreshes them), the standards evidenced, the competency and badges it proves, and deep links that launch each task with its condition. No external host but Google Fonts. `tools/check_tracks.mjs` gates it.

## Content rules for ladder waves
- New stations follow the station brief in full: 12–15 steps, ≥6 kinds, 4 hazards, 2 interruptions, `why` ≥200 characters, ≥5 registry-form authorities, `supportLine`, crew check-in, closing log; eval ≥95 with `std` 100. Two declared interruptions and four hazards are what let a new station serve every band.
- A real person or organisation named in content (a foundation, a founder, a programme) is described only from sources in the repo or reachable on the network at build time; otherwise the content states the principles generically and marks the attribution "not sourced in this repository". That rule produced the Can We Live opener; it applies to every named body.
- Interactions: each new station uses at least one of `drag`, `turn`, `gauge`, `track`.

≤ 300 words: ids, eval rows, mesh counts, what each interruption does, ladder levels filled (the gap table before and after), what failed first.
