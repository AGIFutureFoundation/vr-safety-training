# Ladder brief — ten levels, fifty steps, per programme

Every programme in `WebXR/smartcity/js/curricula.js` becomes a ten-level ladder. This brief binds every task that builds ladder infrastructure or ladder content; `station-brief.md`, `proof-brief.md` and `loop-brief.md` still apply.

## The ladder
- A **level** is an ordered chain of tasks run back to back with one shared score, one results card and one badge. A **task** is a station (an existing one or a new one) run in full. Level N's tasks together contain **at least 50 steps**; a level with fewer is `partial: true` and is listed in `docs/ladders.md` as needing content, never hidden.
- Levels rise: 1–3 orientation and single procedures, 4–6 procedures under interruptions and weather, 7–8 multi-station chains with instructor-injected interruptions, 9 a full shift, 10 a capstone assessed under the mastery rule with zero coaching. Level gates use `shared/competency.js`'s mastery rule; nothing else unlocks a level.
- The ladder is **data** (`WebXR/smartcity/js/ladders.js`, generated by `tools/gen_ladders.mjs` from the programme's stations sorted by difficulty, then hand-tuned entries kept in `ladders.overrides.json`). Checkers gate: ten levels per programme, every task exists, step totals computed from the real station modules, capstone at level 10.
- **Union standards** live in `tools/standards.json`; a level names the standards its tasks evidence (union of its stations' registry hits) and the apprenticeship-hour equivalence only where the registry entry states one. Never invent hours, clauses or a body's endorsement.

## Content rules for ladder waves
- New stations follow the station brief in full: 12–15 steps, ≥6 kinds, 4 hazards, 2 interruptions, `why` ≥200 characters, ≥5 registry-form authorities, `supportLine`, crew check-in, closing log; eval ≥95 with `std` 100.
- A real person or organisation named in content (a foundation, a founder, a programme) is described only from sources in the repo or reachable on the network at build time; otherwise the content states the principles generically and marks the attribution "not sourced in this repository". That rule produced the Can We Live opener; it applies to every named body.
- Interactions: each new station uses at least one of `drag`, `turn`, `gauge`, `track`, and each level 7+ task chain includes at least one instructor-injectable interruption (declared, so `CMD_INTERRUPT` can fire it).

## Hand-back
≤ 250 words: ids, eval rows, mesh counts, what each interruption does, ladder levels filled, what failed first.
