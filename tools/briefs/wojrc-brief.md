## What the edition trains (procedures, all real, cited from `tools/standards.json`)
- **TDL: warehouse** — forklift and pallet jack (OSHA 29 CFR 1910.178), dock and trailer loading, pick/pack and scanning, hazmat labelling (49 CFR 172), racking and load limits, ergonomics (NIOSH lifting).
- **TDL: Commercial Class A** — pre-trip inspection, air-brake test, coupling and uncoupling, backing and docking, cargo securement (49 CFR 393), hours of service and the ELD (49 CFR 395), weigh station and roadside inspection (CVSA), the entry-level driver training rule (49 CFR 380 Subpart F).
- **Apprenticeship navigation** — reading an apprenticeship standard, the application and aptitude test, the tool list and PPE, jobsite orientation, OSHA 10, the union hall and the dispatch list, the first-period evaluation.
- **Financial coaching** — reading a credit report, a debt-reduction plan, a pay stub and withholding, a budget with irregular income, an emergency-savings plan, avoiding predatory lending, benefits and the earned income credit (general facts only, IRS and CFPB as bodies, no tax advice beyond what the cited body's guidance states).
- **Wellness** — stress and sleep for shift work, peer support, the guide's check-in, substance use and the job (SAMHSA), where to ask for help; ties to `shared/ei-guide.js`.
