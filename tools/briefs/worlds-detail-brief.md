# World detail brief — layered maps, interactive assets, fleets and avatars

Binds CARTOGRAPHER (console CARTOGRAPHER). `assets-brief.md`, `console-brief.md` and the facts rule apply; every asset procedural and licence-clean; mesh budgets hold (raise a documented budget only with the measured headroom).

## Build
1. **Layered maps.** Bay World and the Deep gain map layers the in-game map and the Atlas can toggle: roads and transit, job sites by programme colour, landmarks, activities and eggs found, interactive assets, wildlife sightings. Each layer is data (`bayworld-data.js` / `underwater-data.js` gain `*_LAYERS` or the map reads existing data), rendered on the in-game map and the Atlas's SVG fallback.
2. **Interactive assets.** Per zone, set dressing that does something when the learner presses E: an information kiosk that opens the nearest station's programme overview, a bench where the camera sits and the Guide offers a tip, a union hall notice board listing the programmes anchored nearby, a tool crib that shows the station's tools, a bus stop that fast-travels to a visited site, a buoy in the Deep that shows the dive line, a dock box with the yacht briefing. At least 60 across Bay World and 20 in the Deep; each links to a real page through `WebXR/shared/links.js`.
3. **Fleets.** Traffic and harbour fleets gain variety: liveries by service (transit, utility, delivery, emergency, construction, port) with generic names and colours, no real brands; the regatta's twelve yachts keep their names; the Deep's workboats, the ROV and survey vessels get distinct liveries. `check_fleet.mjs` extended for livery coverage.
4. **Avatar styles.** `WebXR/shared/crew.js` figures gain a style system: body proportions (several), skin tones (a wide range), hair styles and head coverings (including hijab, turban and caps), trade PPE by category (hard hat colours, hi-vis, dive gear, chef whites, scrubs, flight crew), and an avatar picker on the account chip that the worlds use for the learner's own figure (stored per profile through `profiles.js`). Respectful and non-stereotyped: no trade is tied to a skin tone, gender or body type.
5. `tools/check_worlds_detail.mjs` in `check_all`: layers present and rendered, interactive asset counts and every asset's link resolves, livery coverage, the avatar style space covered, budgets held.
