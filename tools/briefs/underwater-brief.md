# Underwater world brief — the Deep: a large detailed dive map under the bay

Binds DEEP1 (console TRENCH: the shared world data and builder) and DEEP2 (console REEF: the dive game, quests and eggs). `console-brief.md`, `bayarea-brief.md`'s facts rule and the rules at the head of `WebXR/shared/bayworld-data.js` apply: public place names only, generic descriptions, no date, depth figure, count, owner, event, species claim, organisation or brand; depth, gas, decompression and current limits are never stated ("per the dive plan and the tables the supervisor holds"). No violence, no gambling.

## The shared contract (DEEP1 owns it; DEEP2 builds against it)
`WebXR/shared/underwater-data.js` (pure, no three.js), mirroring bayworld-data.js's shape so the same tools can read it:
- `DEEP_BOUNDS = { minX:-1000, maxX:1000, minZ:-700, maxZ:700 }` (2000 × 1400 m of seabed), `DEEP_DEPTH_RANGE` and `deepDepthAt(x, z)` — a continuous seabed depth field (positive metres below the surface; a shallow shelf, an eelgrass meadow, a kelp forest on rock, a shipping channel, a wreck hollow, pier pilings near the shore, an outfall apron, a tidal-marsh channel mouth, a deep trench and a seamount). The field is used for scenery and gameplay only; the numbers are never shown to a learner as a limit.
- `DEEP_ZONES` (at least 12, `{ id, name, centre:[x,z], radius, palette:{water, seabed, accent}, blurb }`), `deepZoneAt(x, z)` by nearest centre.
- `DEEP_LANDMARKS` (at least 20: a kelp cathedral, a wreck's bow, a pier's piling forest, a reef ball field, an eelgrass meadow edge, a channel marker chain, a tide-gauge post, a seamount pinnacle, a marsh mouth, an outfall diffuser… generic names, `{ id, name, zone, position:[x,z], kind, blurb }`).
- `DEEP_SITES` (at least 30: dive and survey sites, `{ id, name, zone, position:[x,z], programmes:[…], stations:[…] }`), anchoring real programme ids from `WebXR/smartcity/js/curricula.js` and real station ids from the catalog: the `br-`, `mw-`, `eelgrass-transplant`, `hp-`/bay-restoration stations that exist, plus `cd-` and `me-` ids where the brief `dive-brief.md` names them (anchor those with a comment; they join at integration).
- `DEEP_LINES` — dive lines, anchor lines and transects in place of roads (`{ id, name, kind:"transect"|"anchor-line"|"guideline"|"channel", points:[[x,z]…] }`), one connected network; `deepLineAt(x, z)` returns `{ onLine, id, heading }` or null.
- `DEEP_MESH_BUDGET = { high, low }`; `WebXR/shared/underwater.js` exports `buildUnderwater(parent, { detail, zone })` and `deepLighting(band)` (band `"shallow" | "mid" | "deep"`: fog colour and density, caustic light, particulate). High detail builds the whole seabed with kelp stands, eelgrass, reef balls, a wreck, pilings, an outfall, fish schools and a marsh mouth within the budget; low detail is a self-contained vignette for the SmartCiti.X district `the-deep` (register it in `smartcity/js/districts.js` under the scenic budget).
- `tools/check_underwater.mjs`, in `check_all`: bounds, zone coverage, every landmark and site in its declared zone, every programme anchored somewhere in DEEP_SITES or BAY_SITES, connected lines, continuous depth, builds within budget at both details. `docs/underwater.md`.

## The game (DEEP2)
`WebXR/underwater/` (page `underwater.html`, `js/`): swim or pilot an ROV over the seabed with the shared builder, a qualitative reserve gauge (a bar that shrinks; never a number, never a depth readout as a limit), a buddy line, an ascent line to the surface at every site, day light falling off with depth, a career ledger (survey credits and dive reputation), an in-world map from DEEP_BOUNDS, job boards at every site opening the real stations (`../smartcity/dist/smartcity-x.html?sim=…`), and `?site=` deep links. Quest layer: `WebXR/underwater/js/dives.js` + generated `dives-data.js` from `tools/gen_dive_quests.mjs` — a six-dive main arc (a survey career from the pier pilings to the seamount), an opener and a capstone side dive per programme anchored in DEEP_SITES, at least 20 field-note eggs (lantern eggs: a rope knot at a piling, a tide-gauge riddle, a kelp holdfast note — generic, informative, no species fact stated as a claim), and four scored activities (a kelp transect time trial, a wreck photo survey, a debris sweep, a marsh-mouth drift). `tools/check_underwater_game.mjs` walks a main dive and an egg to completion headlessly. Bundle it (`tools/bundle_webxr.py`), link it from the home page and from Bay World's map ("Dive"). Until DEEP1's data lands in your worktree, build against a stub `js/seabed-stub.js` with the exact shape above and switch to the shared module when the coordinator says it is on the branch.

## Process
Start with `git fetch origin claude/vr-ar-safety-training-wkwmve && git merge --ff-only FETCH_HEAD`. DEEP1 commits the data module first, within twenty minutes, before the builder. Commit in steps; each commit message ends with the two trailer lines in your task. Gate: `python3 tools/bundle_webxr.py` and `node tools/check_all.mjs` ending in the exact "All N checkers pass" line. Hand back within 75 minutes: ≤250 words, commit hashes, the check_all line, counts and mesh counts.
