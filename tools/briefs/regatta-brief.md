# Regatta brief — a fleet of individual yachts, hosted events and yacht racing on the open bay

Binds REGATTA (console REGATTA). `console-brief.md`, `assets-brief.md` and `bayarea-brief.md`'s facts rule apply: original yacht names and liveries, no real boat, club, race, sponsor, brand or person; no gambling (no betting, no wagers, no odds); no violence. Right-of-way is taught as the registry's own "33 CFR 83" inland rules read generically ("the give-way vessel keeps clear"), never as a clause number.

## The fleet
`WebXR/shared/yacht-fleet.js` (pure data + a small builder wrapper): `YACHT_FLEET` — twelve individual yachts built on `motorYacht` from `WebXR/shared/fleet.js` (read its `opts`; extend it with `hull`, `trim`, `flybridge` (open|hardtop), `length` scale in a safe range, `name` on the transom via `signFace`/`paintTexture`, a burgee colour) — each with an original name (a word or two, invented, e.g. "Tidewater Lark"), a home berth (a site id from `WebXR/shared/bayworld-data.js`: the island yacht harbour, the estuary marina, the north marina pier, the south shoreline marina), a crew role list and an events list. `buildYacht(parent, id, x, y, z, heading)`, `yachtById(id)`. Each yacht is a variant, not a new builder; the shared `FLEET_BUDGET` does not change.

## Events and racing
`WebXR/regatta/` (page `regatta.html`, `js/`): a race and events app on the Bay World water zones (`outer-bay`, `estuary-waterfront`, the island harbour approaches; read `BAY_ZONES` and `bayRoadAt` is irrelevant on water — write `regattaCourseAt` for the marks). Courses: three original courses laid with turning marks and a start/finish line in `WebXR/regatta/js/courses.js` (data only, positions inside `BAY_BOUNDS` water). Racing: the learner helms one yacht of the fleet against the rest (AI yachts follow the marks with simple steering and spacing), with scoring on time, every mark rounded on the correct side, the no-wake zone held at the harbour mouth, right-of-way kept at crossings (a give-way check like the driving stations' following-distance check), and a clean docking at the finish. Events: `WebXR/regatta/js/events.js` — a hosted-events calendar (an original set: a union family day cruise, a sunset safety cruise, a regatta day, a crew-training day, a harbour clean-up flotilla) where each event runs a briefing (guest count, life jackets, muster point) before the on-water part and pays reputation and credits into the same career ledger as Bay World (`WebXR/bayworld/js/career.js`; read it, do not fork it). Weather and sky: read `WebXR/shared/weather.js` and, when `WebXR/shared/sky.js` exists in your worktree, `skyFor(time, weather)` — wind shifts the AI yachts and the learner's helm; fog shortens the mark visibility. `tools/check_regatta.mjs` in `check_all`: fleet ids unique and each yacht's berth a real site, courses inside bounds on water, a headless race walked to the finish with the checks scored, events reference real stations for their briefings (`yc-` ids). Bundle it, link it from the home page and from Bay World's map and the yacht harbour job board ("Regatta").

## Process
Start with `git fetch origin claude/vr-ar-safety-training-wkwmve && git merge --ff-only FETCH_HEAD`. Keep `docs/consoles/REGATTA.md`. The bundler concatenates every module into one scope and erases import aliases: prefix top-level names (`rg…`, `RG_…`). Commit in steps; each commit message ends with the two trailer lines in your task. Gate with `python3 tools/bundle_webxr.py` and `node tools/check_all.mjs` (exact "All N checkers pass" line). Hand back within 45 minutes: ≤250 words, commit hashes, the check_all line, fleet/course/event counts.
