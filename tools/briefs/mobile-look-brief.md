# Mobile look brief — textures, colours and patterns that read well on a phone

Binds PALETTE (console PALETTE: the texture and pattern library and its use in the open worlds) and TOUCH (console TOUCH: the mobile play surface). `console-brief.md` applies. Nothing is downloaded: every texture stays procedural (canvas-painted in `WebXR/shared/textures.js`) so the bundles stay self-contained and licence-clean.

## PALETTE — the pattern library and the worlds
1. `WebXR/shared/textures.js`: add a pattern set painted at the `TEXTURE_RES` tier from `perf.js` (low tier ≤ 256 px, mid 512, high 1024), each a named painter in `PAINTER_IDS` with a manifest slot: `teakDeck` (yacht deck planking with caulk seams), `hullStripe` (boot-top and sheer stripes, colour from opts), `nonSkid` (diamond non-skid), `harbourWater` (layered ripples and a sparkle band), `causticSeabed` (caustic light over sand), `kelpBlade`, `reefRock`, `crosswalk`, `laneAsphalt` (worn lane paint), `brickPaver`, `tileMosaic`, `corrugatedRoof`, `glassCurtainWall` (mullions and reflections), `stuccoWarm`, `turfStripe`, `sailCloth`, `canopyFabric`, `rustPatina`, `safetyChevron`, `signageEnamel`. Add palettes: `bayworld-day`, `bayworld-dusk`, `bayworld-night`, `regatta`, `deep-shallow`, `deep-mid`, `deep-deep`, and a colour-blind-safe accent set (check contrast of accents against the palette's ground ≥ 3:1 in a small function the checker calls).
2. Apply them: Bay World's builder (`WebXR/shared/bayworld.js`) gives buildings per-zone facade patterns (glass curtain wall downtown, warm stucco and tile in the market district, corrugated and brick in the industrial flank, painted wood at the marinas), roads get lane asphalt and crosswalks, water gets harbour water; the yacht (`motorYacht`, `yachtTender` in `fleet.js`) gets teak deck, non-skid and hull stripes from its livery opts; the regatta fleet (`yacht-fleet.js`) passes each yacht's colours through; the Deep (`underwater.js`) gets caustic seabed, kelp blades and reef rock by depth band; Fairway Park gets turf stripes. Every material must still merge within the existing mesh budgets (textures only, no new meshes except where a budget has headroom).
3. Mobile economy: one shared texture cache keyed by painter + res + palette so a world never paints the same canvas twice; `textureStats()` reporting canvases and total pixels; low tier on a phone must stay under 12 megapixels of canvas in Bay World high detail.
4. `tools/check_textures.mjs` (extend): every new painter paints at all three tiers headlessly, deterministic by seed, the cache hits on repeat, the pixel budget holds, the contrast function passes for every palette.
5. Screenshots for the coordinator are not required; the coordinator records them.

## TOUCH — the mobile play surface
1. One shared touch layer `WebXR/shared/touch.js` used by Bay World, the Regatta, the Deep and Fairway Park (read their current touch code first and converge on it): a left virtual stick, context buttons on the right sized ≥ 48 px, safe-area insets respected, haptics via `navigator.vibrate` when present, a one-time gesture hint.
2. Auto quality: on a coarse pointer or a small screen, `perf.js` picks the low tier (resolution scale, shadow off, fog nearer, fewer wildlife and traffic agents) unless the viewer chose otherwise; a small quality toggle in each HUD (Low / Balanced / High) persisted in localStorage with try/catch.
3. HUD responsiveness: every game HUD reflows at 360 × 640 portrait and 640 × 360 landscape with no overlap between the minimap, the reserve or score panel and the touch controls; text ≥ 14 px; the menus scroll inside their own panel.
4. `tools/check_mobile.mjs` in `check_all`: headless Chromium (Playwright at `/opt/node22/lib/node_modules/playwright`, executable `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium`, pages from `WebXR/dist/`) opens each game at both phone sizes with touch emulation, starts it, asserts no overlap between the HUD's fixed panels (bounding boxes), the stick and buttons exist and are ≥ 48 px, the low tier was picked, and no page error. If the checker cannot launch a browser, it fails with a clear message rather than passing.

## Process (both teams)
Start with `git fetch origin claude/vr-ar-safety-training-wkwmve && git merge --ff-only FETCH_HEAD`. Keep your console file. The bundler concatenates every module into one scope and erases import aliases: prefix new top-level names (`tx…` for PALETTE, `tc…` for TOUCH). Commit in steps; each commit message ends with the two trailer lines in your task. Gate with `python3 tools/bundle_webxr.py` and `node tools/check_all.mjs` (exact "All N checkers pass" line). Hand back within 40 minutes: ≤200 words, commit hashes, the check_all line, and (PALETTE) the pixel budget measured, (TOUCH) the pages covered.
