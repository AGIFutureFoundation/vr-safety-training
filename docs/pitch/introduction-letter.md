**Introducing the SmartCiti.X ~ Holodeck prototype, and an invitation to collaborate**

I am writing on behalf of AGI Future Foundation to introduce a working prototype we have built, and to ask whether you would be open to a conversation about shaping its next stage with us.

SmartCiti.X ~ Holodeck is a training platform for union trades that runs in a phone browser, on a laptop and in a headset from one build. At its front is a free-roam game: a stylised Bay Area city, a harbour with a racing fleet, an underwater dive world and a golf and sports park. Every job board in that game opens a graded procedure written to the standards the trades' own training bodies publish. Today the prototype holds 630 such procedures in 54 programmes, drawing on a registry of 111 unions, and every change to it passes 57 automated checks before it is kept.

One programme was built with a specific organisation's published work in mind. wojrc.org describes its programmes in its own words as training "for warehouse and Commercial A truck driver positions", help to "navigate and enroll in union construction trades apprenticeship programs", and "financial coaching to help build your credit score, reduce debt, and build savings". Our Job Readiness Edition turns each of those lines into practice stations, and our Pathway Edition follows a participant from intake and a mock interview through a warehouse ride-along, a permit test, apprenticeship enrolment and a first paycheck, to graduation and an alumni mentor. Joyce Guy has been named by the sponsor of this edition as the person who runs those programmes. We have stated nothing about the organisation beyond its own published text, and we would welcome its staff reviewing every station before any learner sees it.

We are looking for collaborators in four roles:

1. **Workforce programmes** willing to pilot an edition with a cohort and tell us what works in their setting.
2. **Union training bodies** willing to review the stations for their trade and correct anything that does not match how the work is done.
3. **Employers** willing to help shape the ride-along, job-fair and first-day scenes so they reflect real hiring.
4. **Funders** interested in supporting a pilot of [scope and term].

A private link to the prototype, a three-page overview and a feature on the Pathway Edition are enclosed. I would be glad to walk you through it in thirty minutes at a time that suits you, or to arrange a hands-on session for your team.

Thank you for considering it.
