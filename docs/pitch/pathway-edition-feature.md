# Feature: the Pathway Edition and the Job Readiness Edition for wojrc.org

*A deep feature on the two SmartCiti.X ~ Holodeck programmes built around the work wojrc.org describes. Every figure below is read from the platform's own catalog, eval report and world data on 2026-09-27; every statement about the organisation is its own published text.*

## What the organisation says it does

The only facts this platform states about the organisation are the words it publishes about itself on wojrc.org, as supplied by the sponsor of this edition:

> Everyone who is willing to work hard deserves an opportunity to succeed. For the past 14 years, we have been helping low-income Bay Area residents' gain the skills and confidence they need to get and keep jobs, and build financial security that support themselves and their families.
>
> How We Help: We offer training for warehouse and Commercial A truck driver positions. We prepare you and help you navigate and enroll in union construction trades apprenticeship programs. We offer financial coaching to help build your credit score, reduce debt, and build savings.

Two of its pages are titled "TDL Pre-Apprenticeship Training" and "Wellness Resource Center". Joyce Guy is named by the sponsor of this edition as the person who runs the programmes. Nothing else about the organisation, its staff, its outcomes or its history is stated here, because nothing else is sourced.

## Two editions, one pathway

The platform turns each line of that text into graded procedures a learner can practise on a phone, in a browser or in a headset.

| | Job Readiness Edition | Pathway Edition |

| Programme id | `job-readiness-edition` | `wojrc-pathway-edition` |
| Stations | 32 | 40 |
| Stations of its own | the shared roster | 8 pathway milestones plus the full Job Readiness roster |

The **Job Readiness Edition** mirrors the four things the organisation says it offers, station for station:

- **Warehouse training (TDL):** forklift and dock load-out, pallet jack and racking, pick-pack-and-scan, trailer loading and the dock plate, hazmat labelling and segregation, lifting scored the way the Revised NIOSH Lifting Equation scores it.
- **Commercial Class A:** pre-trip inspection, the in-cab air-brake test, coupling and uncoupling, backing and docking, cargo securement and hours of service, then six driven stations from city turns and a freeway merge to a mountain grade, night fog at a rail crossing, a backing serpentine, and the terminal's light-vehicle and forklift check-rides.
- **Apprenticeship navigation:** reading a registered apprenticeship standard, the application and aptitude test, jobsite orientation and what an OSHA 10 card means, the union hall and the dispatch slip, the first-period evaluation, and a heritage opener that frames the trades as a lineage and says plainly what it does not know.
- **Financial coaching:** reading a credit report and disputing its errors, a debt-reduction plan, the first pay stub and the W-4, a budget built on the lean month, an emergency fund and reading a loan offer by its APR.
- **Wellness** (the Wellness Resource Center page's subject): shift work, sleep and stress; a peer-support conversation; substance use and the job; asking for help and matching each problem to its door.

The **Pathway Edition** wraps that roster in the milestones a participant actually passes through, each a full station of its own:

Every milestone station has 12 to 14 steps across eight kinds of interaction, four hazards and two interruptions that change the scene, the same bar as every trade station on the platform.

## Where it lives in the game

Both editions are anchored in Bay World, the platform's free-roam city. The Pathway Edition's job board sits at the Port Container Terminal; the Job Readiness Edition is anchored at the distribution sites where its warehouse and driving stations already stand. Bay World's seven-quest main arc follows the edition's shape from start to finish: "Where the Trades Began", "First Day on the Floor", "Yard Qualified", "On the Road", "Sign the Book", "Balance the Books" and "Ask for the Door". Each programme also carries an opener and a capstone side quest, and reputation and credits earned in the city are kept in the same career ledger the learner's training records use.

## Standards it is written to

The stations cite only authorities held in the platform's standards registry, in their registry form: 29 CFR 1910.178 for powered industrial trucks, 49 CFR 172 for hazmat communication, 49 CFR 380 Subpart F for entry-level driver training, 49 CFR 392, 393 and 395 for driving, securement and hours of service, 29 CFR 1926.21 for site orientation, the NIOSH lifting equation, the IRS and the CFPB as bodies for the financial stations, and SAMHSA's trauma-informed guidance for wellness. The Pathway competency also names the HIPAA privacy rule for the intake desk.

## What a partner would see in a pilot

- A cohort can run the whole pathway from a phone or a classroom browser; a headset is optional.
- An instructor console shows each learner's attempts, stars, unsafe actions and interruptions handled, and the training records export as CSV and xAPI.
- Every station can be reviewed line by line by the partner's own staff before a cohort sees it; the facts rule means nothing about the organisation can appear that it has not published itself.
- The same content exports to Unity for a partner who prefers a native build.

## What is not claimed

No outcome, placement rate, cohort size, funding or endorsement is stated or implied. This is a working prototype built around the organisation's published description of its programmes, offered as the starting point for a conversation.
