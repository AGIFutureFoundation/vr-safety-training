Dental Careers & Dental Hygiene — Unspoken Smiles

Two 19-station training programmes that turn the roles of a dental clinic into scored procedures a learner runs in a browser or a headset. Every run is scored and recorded, and the steps cite the guidance they stand on.

Prepared for the Unspoken Smiles team. This page describes only the programmes as they stand in the SmartCiti.X repository at commit ce62330 (2026-09-28).

Dental Hygiene — Unspoken Smiles

Eighteen stations that make a hygienist's clinical day — the operatory, the sterilisation centre, the chairside procedures, the emergencies and the outreach van — into scored procedures a training programme can run and record. Every step names the guideline it stands on. (The catalog summary counts the 18 Dental & Oral Health stations; the nineteenth is the Trade Skills Draw Station.)

The bodies above are named only as the catalog names them. The prototype is not accredited, endorsed or reviewed by any of them. Infection-control, radiography and sedation settings stay "per the clinic protocol" or "per the manufacturer's instructions", exactly as the stations say.

What a learner does

A learner opens a station, is briefed, and works the procedure step by step in a 3D clinic room: selecting the right instrument or control, putting a sequence in order, holding a gauge in its band, dragging an item to where it belongs, or finding every item on a checklist. The interaction kinds across the 37 stations are select, find, turn, drag, gauge, sequence, hold, track. Registered hazards sit in the room, and interruptions fire mid-procedure (a parent walks in, a spore test comes back positive, a waterline runs dry) and must be answered.

How a run is graded

★★★ ★★ ★

3 stars: no corrections at all and finished inside par. 2 stars: at most one correction and finished inside 1.5x par. 1 star: anything else. Stars, not score, decide whether a run counts.

Unsafe actions

Touching a registered hazard is recorded as an unsafe action. A wrong or unanswered interruption counts as one too. A run with any unsafe action does not count, whatever it scored.

Mastery

A run demonstrates mastery when it earns two or more stars, records zero unsafe actions, answers every interruption it was given, and finishes within 1.5 times the station's par time. One mastery run on enough of a competency's stations earns "demonstrated"; mastery runs on three different days earn "consistent". No other rule earns a competency.

The ladder

Each programme is also a twenty-level ladder. A lesson is one station step run under one condition (daylight, dusk, hazard mode, a named interruption, or the no-hints assessment variant), and each level holds at least 75 lessons. Level 1 is Orientation with the easiest procedures as authored and coached; level 20 is a capstone with no coaching.

Where the programmes live in the open worlds

Bay World · Fruitvale Community College (fruitvale-community-college) in the Fruitvale zone anchors both programmes. Its job board offers Draw Station, Dental Careers Pathway, Four-Handed Dentistry, alongside the k12-science programme.

SmartCiti.X · every dental station is an indoor clinic room rather than a street district.

The Deep · no underwater site anchors either programme.

Bay World quests · 4 side quests, an opener and a capstone for each programme:

  • Dental Hygiene — Unspoken Smiles — First Shift (opener, given by the programme's training lead)
    Stations: Draw Station, Operatory Turnover, Instrument Reprocessing · reward 100 XP and a badge
  • Dental Hygiene — Unspoken Smiles — Capstone (capstone, given by the programme's certifying evaluator)

  • Dental Careers — Unspoken Smiles — First Shift (opener, given by the programme's training lead)
    Stations: Patient Intake Screening, Dental Careers Pathway, Four-Handed Dentistry · reward 100 XP and a badge
  • Dental Careers — Unspoken Smiles — Capstone (capstone, given by the programme's certifying evaluator)
    Stations: Digital Intraoral Scanning & CAD/CAM, Dental Trauma & Avulsed Tooth Response, Dental Coding, Billing & Pre-authorisation · reward 250 XP and a badge

FlowHub · dental-careers-orientation, “Dental Careers — Orientation (Unspoken Smiles)”. The programme is the orientation; the branch after it is the point. A three-star intake sends the student on to the chairside and radiography work an assistant does; anything less sends them back through the shared first ten minutes before they choose a direction.

The Guide · a help button on every page answers typed or spoken questions from a knowledge base generated from the repository. It holds 39 entries for these programmes and their stations, plus one for Fruitvale Community College with a “take me there” link into Bay World.
