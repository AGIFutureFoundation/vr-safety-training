## What is here

  construction robotics, robot maintenance and lockout, data and AI-training roles — × five levels (awareness / K-12 → robot
  operator → technician → integrator / safety lead → AI-training specialist), each level's stations listed explicitly and its
  competency candidates; `RP_ROBOT_STATIONS` (the honest list of stations where a robot is the subject of the practice);
  `RP_STANDARDS` (ISO 10218-1, ISO 10218-2, ISO/TS 15066, ANSI/A3 R15.06, OSHA 1910.147 — named only, no clause text);
  `RP_LOOP` (consent → demonstrate → train by behaviour cloning → evaluate on held-out seeds → the robot demonstrates back).
- `WebXR/shared/rp-programme.js` (imports only competency.js): ladders with capstones, the coverage eval, the guarded loop,
  DEAN templates with instructor guides, cohort set-up on the org layer.
- `tools/gen_robotics_programme.mjs` → `WebXR/robotics/programme.html` (design system, Home chip, Guide, cohort form) and
  `docs/robotics-programme.md` (handbook with all 30 instructor guides). Linked from the homepage (`#hm-robotics-programme`).
- `tools/check_robotics_programme.mjs` — the gate (in check_all's list and the checker baseline; never run from here).
- Four gap stations (below).

## Eval — robot-station coverage of each pathway level
A level is covered when one of its own stations (capstones not counted) is a robot station; an AI-training level also needs a
loop station (demonstration collection or policy review). Measured by `rpCoverage()` with and without the new stations.
- Before (existing catalog only): **15/30** — aware 0/6, operator 5/6, technician 5/6, integrator 5/6, ai-training 0/6.
  Gaps: no K-12 robot lesson, no construction robot station, no station for collecting demonstrations or reviewing a policy.
- After: **30/30** — every level 6/6.

## New stations (eval_content)
- `rp-teleop-demonstration-collection` — **98**. Recording teleoperation demonstrations on a cobot bench: consent read first,
  risk assessment, reduced-speed mode, e-stop and scanner proven, nobody else in the camera's frame, grip inside its band,
  failed takes labelled failed, data on this device, revoke deletes. Interruptions: visitor in the zone → bench e-stop (lamp +
  material swap); over-force → release (lamp + gripper moved).
- `rp-robot-policy-evaluation-review` — **98**. Dataset card and consent, lineage, the stale policy built on revoked data,
  threshold from the written criteria, results between the random baseline and the scripted expert on held-out seeds,
  violations, the failing episode replayed and explained (heuristic), stale tag withdrawn, supervised trial on an enabling
  device, sign-off with a rollback. Interruptions: person on the zone tape → e-stop (figure shown/moved); explanation vs scanner
  mismatch → pause trial (material swaps).
- `rp-construction-drilling-robot-setup` — **99**. Ceiling-drilling robot on a concrete deck: work plan, deck scan for
  conduit/tendons, barricade the reach, position on control points, dust collection (29 CFR 1926.1153 Table 1 named for
  drilling), the conflicting hole removed, enable held, vacuum flow watched, bit changed with the battery isolated, HEPA
  cleanup. Interruptions: walker under the mast → remote e-stop; vacuum clog → stop drill (dust plume shown/hidden).
- `k12-rp-how-a-robot-knows-to-stop` — **96** (generated by `tools/gen_k12_station.mjs` from `tools/k12-data/rp-robot-stop.json`).
  Sensors, zones, who restarts a robot, robot jobs; check_k12 passes.
