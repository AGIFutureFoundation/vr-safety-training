# Console SECONDLINE — gamify the five New Orleans parishes

Team: SECONDLINE · Brief: `$SP/crescent/crescent-brief.md` (SECONDLINE section, Shared rules, the Facts rule for New Orleans, the Shared data contract) with `tools/briefs/console-brief.md` and `tools/briefs/frontier-brief.md` · Branch: `worktree-agent-acd833dcadd85df26` off the coordinator's tree (`01539ab`).

Rules held: no invented history, dates, statistics, populations, addresses or business names about New Orleans — a parish, a neighbourhood, a bridge, a causeway, a ferry, a park, a port, a campus, a stadium district are named only as places; every gate id resolves; no violence, gambling, loot boxes or purchases; every new top-level name prefixed `sl`/`SL_`; nothing in this console's data carries a coordinate — positions belong to PARISH's and DELTA's `np-data-<parish>.js`.

## Plan (fixed before code)

- **One data module, `WebXR/shared/sl-parish-play.js`**, keyed by parish id (`orleans`, `jefferson`, `st-bernard`, `plaquemines`, `st-tammany`) and site id (kebab-case, kind-derived: Orleans carries the PARISH section's ten kinds as `port-terminal`, `levee-crew`, `pumping-station`, `streetcar-barn`, `rail-yard`, `hospital-district`, `university-campus`, `stadium-district`, `hospitality-row`, `wetlands-restoration`). Each site names existing catalog stations by trade and union ids from `tools/unions.json`. Because PARISH and DELTA choose their own site ids in parallel, every SL site also carries a `match` pattern and `slResolveSite(parishData, id)` resolves by id first, then by id/name/kind — the merge is additive and a renamed site still binds.
- **Main arc `SL_MAIN_QUESTS`** — storm-season readiness in seven chained quests: the levee walk, the pumping station, the port, the hospital, the river road south (floodgate, refinery corridor, the ferry, the pilot station), across the river bridge to Jefferson's drainage pumps and airport, over the causeway to the north-shore staging yard. Steps: `goto`, `talk` (a job title, never a name), `station`, `lesson`, `cross` (a connector, resolved by kind and the two parishes), `game`. Rewards are badges and XP tiers; nothing purchasable.
- **Side games `SL_SIDE_GAMES` (35)** in the side-game contract (`kind: "side-game"`, `world: "parishes"`, `parish`, `site`, `siteName`, `task`, `gate`, `practices`, `mechanic`, `reward.cosmetic`), five to ten per parish, every one of the twelve mechanics used, one K-12 gate. Exported as `SL_GATED` for `check_gates`.
- **Treasures**: `tools/gen_treasures.mjs` gains a `parishes` surface — a Storm Kit Cache at every site (lesson: the site's own station why or a union registry note, verbatim) and a quiet Field Scholar find for every parish field lesson. A parish treasure's trigger is **site-relative** (`{ world: "parishes", parish, site, dx, dz, r }`): `treasures.js`'s `tzWatchWorld` gains an optional `at(trigger)` resolver and `slTreasureAt(parishData)` supplies it, so no coordinate is ever written here.
- **Field lessons `SL_FIELD_LESSONS` (30)** on the Redwood shape plus `parish` and `station` (the trade station): five or six per parish, every K-12 programme reached, no digit, reading level inside the K-12 ceiling.
- **Choose your path**: `slPathBoard(parishId, opts)` (pure) and `slMountPathBoard(el, parishId, opts)` (DOM, no three.js) — trade (the parish's stations by site), classroom (its field lessons), or just play (its side games with lock state, its treasure count).
- **NPC hand-offs** for GRIOT: `SL_HANDOFFS` (one per site per kind: station, lesson, game, treasure hint; giver = a job title) and the hook `slHandoffsFor({ parish, site })` / `slHandoffTarget(h)` → `{ kind, id, title, href }`. Every line is a title re-read verbatim from this module (`source`).
- **Checkers**: extend `check_gates.mjs` (import `SL_GATED`, 25+ items over five parishes, each placed at an SL site, the parishes app checked only when `WebXR/parishes/js/app.js` exists), `check_treasures.mjs` (`parishes` floor 40, site-relative triggers, parish field-lesson finds), `check_k12.mjs` (8d: the parish lessons), `gen_gate_names.mjs` (reads `SL_GATED`), and a new `tools/check_parish_play.mjs` in `check_all` (the arc, the boards, the hand-offs, the facts denylist, resolution against `np-data-*.js` when present).
