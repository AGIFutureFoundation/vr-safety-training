# PARISH memory — read this before touching the parish engine

Short, durable lessons for the next team at this console (the Crescent brief's PARISH section, docs/consoles/PARISH.md).

- **The engine is two halves.** `shared/np-parish.js` is pure (height field, water, levees, chunks, massing, triangulation, validator) and `shared/np-world.js` is the three.js builder over it; `np-geo.js` is the affine fit; `np-parishes.js` the registry. A parish module (`np-data-<id>.js`) is data only. Keep it that way: the checker builds the world headless on the vendored three.js (`WebXR/vendor/three/dist/three.module.min.js` imports in Node with no DOM), which is exact where a stub would guess.
- **Write the map from lon/lat, not by hand.** Orleans was generated by a scratch script (`$SP/crescent/parish/gen_orleans.mjs`: approximate public lon/lat → `[x, z]` through one affine, `x = (lon − lonC)·kx`, `z = (latC − lat)·kz`, kz = kx·1.149 so the map keeps the real aspect). Every feature then shares the anchors' fit (residual 0.4 m). When a river or a canal moves, everything that hugs it moves with it — the levees are `offset(river, ±118)`, not typed.
- **A water `poly` with `width` is a centreline.** The engine widens it into a strip (`npStripFromCentreline`) and everything — `npWaterAt`, the height cut, the massing exclusion, the site and road checks — reads the strip. The first Orleans river sat 300 m too far inland and put four sites, three landmarks and Magazine Street in the water; move the centreline, not the sites.
- **Roads may cross a canal, a bayou or a wetland on a short span; never a river, lake or gulf without `kind: "bridge"`, `"causeway"` or `"ferry"`.** `npRoadWet` is the rule; `npRoadSurfaceAt` lifts a plain road to a flat span over spannable water. A bridge deck ramps over the first and last fifth (`npDeckHeightAt`) and gets piers wherever it stands 2.5 m clear.
- **Connectors are paired by id and the owner never guesses the other frame.** Write `to: { parish, position: null, lonlat }` for a parish that is not built; `npResolveConnectors` projects it through the other parish's fit at load, and the checker reports the connector as *pending* until that module exists, then holds both ends to 1 km and prints the `to.position` that would close a gap. An in-parish ferry is judged as a crossing (a real gap over water), not by the 1 km rule — at a 3.4× stylised scale a 340 m crossing is 1.2 real km.
- **Facts checks by words, not digits.** Coordinates are four-digit numbers, so a "no year" regex fails on every map; ordinals in public names (17th Street Canal) are names. The check reads the site, landmark and district names and blurbs, and looks for history words.
- **The way back is `#site=<parish>/<site>`.** `lkStationLink` encodes the site id and then the whole return, so a check must expect `encodeURIComponent("#site=" + encodeURIComponent("orleans/port-terminal"))`. The app reads `?parish=`, else the parish half of a return's site, else the first parish.
- **Budget numbers (Orleans).** High tier: 49 chunks, 171 meshes / 70 k triangles at the worst site, ~850 massing instances; low: 25 chunks, 118 / 44 k. The pure worst-case estimate is 101 k / 55 k against NP_BUDGET 400 k and 260 draw calls. A headless build takes ~4 s (massing per chunk is the cost); the browser builds the first 49 chunks in one go at start, then two per frame.
- **Wildlife kinds live in the shared table.** `egrets` (wade, 4 meshes each) and `herons` (glide on `wlBird`) were added to `WILDLIFE_BUDGET` with `total` raised to 128; `check_sky` builds every kind and holds it to its budget, so a new kind needs its `count × meshes` in `total`.
- **Wiring a new world page, the full list.** Bundler `APPS` (auth.js and account.js in its list — `check_home` counts them against `"modules": [`) and `DIST_PAGES`; `gen_seo.mjs` `WF_APPS` (its sitemap entry needs the flat file, so write `WebXR/dist/<page>` through `combined_fixup` when you cannot afford the full bundle); `gen_home.mjs` layout tables and `appCard`; `check_home.mjs` `HM_WORLDS` and a ≤ 60 KB JPEG at `WebXR/home/img/<id>.jpg` (`HM_ONLY=<id> node tools/capture_home_thumbs.mjs` serves `WebXR/dist`); `capture_home_thumbs.mjs` `HM_CAPTURES`; `passport.js` `PP_APP_NAMES`; `links.js` `LK_ASSET_WORLDS`; `field-lessons.js` `K2_WORLD_PAGES`; `check_all`.
- **The full combined `WebXR/dist/`** is only rebuilt by a full `python3 tools/bundle_webxr.py`; `bundle_webxr.py parishes` refreshes `WebXR/parishes/dist/` only. The bundler's `stamp_seo` rewrites the page's `<head>` on every build — expect `parishes.html` to change on disk after a bundle.
