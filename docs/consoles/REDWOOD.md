# Console REDWOOD — Redwood Reach, the forest world

Brief: `tools/briefs/frontier-brief.md` (REDWOOD section). Memory: `docs/consoles/memory/REDWOOD.md`.
Next brief: `tools/briefs/next/redwood-next.md`. Stills: `docs/img/redwood/`.

## What shipped
- **`WebXR/redwood/redwood.html`** — a walkable forest world, 4096 m × 4096 m (x, z in −2048…2048; north is −z), bundled to
  `WebXR/redwood/dist/redwood.html` and flat as `WebXR/dist/redwood.html`.
- **Terrain** (`js/rw-data.js`): seeded value-noise hills and ridges, higher to the north-east; a coastline along the south edge; a
  river from the north-east highlands winding south-west through a broad valley to an estuary; a summit knob for the lookout; level
  pads for every site. Pure and headless (`rwHeightAt`, `rwIsWater`, `rwBiomeAt`, `rwRiverNearest`).
- **Streaming** (`js/rw-world.js`): 16 × 16 chunks of 256 m. A ring of chunks around the player (radius 2 low/balanced, 3 high) is
  built nearest-first, two per frame, and disposed when left behind. A coarse horizon mesh covers the whole field under the fog.
  Per chunk: one vertex-coloured ground mesh with analytic normals and up to three `InstancedMesh`es (redwood, fir, broadleaf).
  Budget table `RW_BUDGET`; measured at the fire station on high: 49 chunks, ~8.3k tree instances, ~100k ground triangles.
- **Sky, wildlife, day/night**: the shared `sky.js` dome and recipe; `T` advances the clock three hours (day / dusk / night bands
  drive the dome, fog, sun and hemisphere light). Shorebirds, pelicans, gulls and seals at the estuary and coast (`wildlife.js`).
- **Eleven sites** with job boards (`lkStationLink`, `from=redwood`, the way home on every link): Wildland Fire Station, Ridge Fire
  Lookout, Valley Sawmill & Log Yard, Watershed Restoration Reach, Fern Hollow Campground & Trailhead, Forest Nursery & Seed Bank,
  Rural Substation & Line Corridor, Estuary Field Station, Fire Road Equipment Yard, Trail Crew Spike Camp, Old-Growth Grove —
  37 station slots over existing catalog stations (wildland fire, heavy equipment, grounds tree crews, restoration, linemen,
  conveyor/lockout, nursery). No new stations were added.
- **Quests** (`js/rw-career.js`): a six-quest main arc (fire station → lookout → roads → river → mill → estuary) and ten side quests,
  each gated on a real station with the shared gate contract (`gate: { stations, note }`, exported by `rwGatedItems()`); locks show
  the reason and a link to each missing station; the quest log has a "Skills to unlock" panel. Cosmetic rewards only.
- **36 field tins** (the eggs; generated by `tools/gen_redwood.mjs`): one at each landmark, each quoting the first sentence of a real
  programme station's `why` verbatim, in eleven themed site sets (a completed set earns a badge).
- **10 K-12 field lessons**, each anchored at a site and landmark, tied to a `k12-…` catalog station and to the trade here that uses
  the idea. Schema: `{ id, title, site, landmark, k12, trade, tradeLine, minutes, steps[2–4], check: { q, options, answer } }`.
- **Three scored activities**: Trail-Crew Route (4 points), Fuel-Break Survey (5), River-Restoration Count (4) — scored on the safe
  call at each point; no number is ever stated as a limit.
- **Map**: a shaded relief map with seven toggleable layers (sites, roads, trails, water, lessons, quests & locks, found tins), a
  minimap, and fast travel to every visited site. `?site=<id>` and `#site=<id>` deep links land at a site (and reopen its board).
- **Wiring**: bundler entry and flat page; homepage card (`tools/gen_home.mjs`, thumbnail `WebXR/home/img/redwood.jpg`, now eight
  world cards); `redwood-career-v1` is profile-private; `redwood` is a passport source ("Back to Redwood Reach");
  `tools/check_redwood.mjs` in `check_all` (395 checks).
