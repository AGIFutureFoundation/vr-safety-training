## The state programme
- **FastSites** (Site Investment and Infrastructure Fund): created by the Legislature in 2025 with $150 million; $50 million added in
  2026, for $200 million total. Invests in roads, rail, utilities and environmental work at sites; awards from $500,000 to $25 million;
  recipients repay the state as land is sold or leased. Round II: $50 million, applications Oct 1 – Dec 1. Examples: $10 million to extend
  road access at the ~1,000-acre Esperanza site (St. Charles Parish); $1.4 million for McLeod Business Park (Lafourche Parish);
  $5.9 million at the Port of Vinton (Calcasieu Parish: a 600 ft × 50 ft barge berth). Sources: opportunitylouisiana.gov/fastsites;
  kedm.org 2026-09-16; kplctv.com 2026-05-21.
- Louisiana reached a record **2.01 million nonfarm jobs** in May 2026; the Baton Rouge metro added about **7,900** nonfarm jobs May 2025 –
  May 2026 (1.8%). Sources: louisianaradionetwork.com 2026-07-15; wbrz.com; brla.gov newsflash 1738.
