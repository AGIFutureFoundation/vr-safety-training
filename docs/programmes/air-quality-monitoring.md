## About this programme

The air a neighbourhood breathes is measured by a short chain of people and instruments, from a fence-line monitor to a stack test to a certified eye reading a plume, each one held to a different standard for a different reason. This block runs that chain end to end, from where the readings are taken to where the emissions actually come from.

**Unions:** AFSCME air-district technicians, LIUNA environmental laborers, IUOE and USW plant crews

**Certification line:** EPA Method 9 visible emissions (40 CFR 60 Appendix A), EPA 40 CFR Part 58 ambient air quality monitoring, the Bay Area Air Quality Management District's own regulations, and a site's Air Monitoring Plan under a cleanup order
