## About this programme

Load, radius, chart, and what is under the load. Five lifts in five industries that all fail the same way.

**Unions:** Ironworkers, IUOE crane operators, ILWU and IATSE riggers

**Certification line:** ASME B30 rigging and crane practice, NCCCO operator knowledge, and ETCP entertainment rigging for the theatre station
