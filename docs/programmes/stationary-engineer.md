## About this programme

The four plants a building engineer is responsible for at two in the morning, and the tests that prove each one will work when it is needed.

**Unions:** IUOE — International Union of Operating Engineers, stationary locals

**Certification line:** State stationary engineer licence, ASME boiler practice, ASHRAE 188 water management and NFPA 25 fire-pump testing
