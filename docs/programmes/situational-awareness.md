## About this programme

Twenty-two procedures that interrupt you while you work. Every station in this block is one you may already know the order of — the block is not testing the order. It is testing whether you notice the alarm, the person in the wrong place or the thing that moved while your hands and eyes were somewhere else. Miss one and it scores as an unsafe action, because that is what it is.

**Unions:** Cross-craft — run as a refresher block by IBEW, UA, LIUNA, Ironworkers and IAFF locals

**Certification line:** OSHA 1926.20(b)(2) competent-person hazard recognition and the human-factors component every one of these standards assumes: NFPA 70E, 1910.146 permit spaces, 1926.651 excavations, 1926.1400 cranes, NFPA 25 impairment control
