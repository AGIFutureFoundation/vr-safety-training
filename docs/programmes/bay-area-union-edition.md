## About this programme

One Bay Area tool for four union families: the sheet metal shop and the duct run, the Golden Gate Bridge tower, cable and paint programme, the terminal's cranes, fenders and reefer power, and the water itself: ferries, mooring, oil transfer, and the dive stage down to the piles and the hull.

**Unions:** SMART sheet metal workers, the Ironworkers and IUPAT bridge crews with the Pile Drivers of the Carpenters, ILWU longshore and maintenance workers with the PMA training programme and IUOE crane maintenance, and the marine unions: the Inlandboatmen's Union of the ILWU, MEBA and SIU, with the Pile Drivers' commercial divers for the underwater work

**Certification line:** SMART's International Training Institute curriculum, Ironworkers IMPACT and OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart R and Subpart M, IUPAT FTI and SSPC practice for bridge coatings, ILWU-PMA training and OSHA 29 CFR 1917 marine terminals, USCG Subchapter M and 33 CFR 156 for the marine side, and OSHA 29 CFR 1910 Subpart T with ADCI consensus standards for commercial diving

## Competency rule

**Work the Bay Area's sheet metal, bridge, port maintenance and marine trades to their unions' standards** — demonstrated by a mastery run on 6 of its 42 stations. Programme completion: every station has a passing attempt (stars >= 2, no unsafe action).
