## About this programme

The entry point for a youth club's coaches and mentors: a session started with the floor checked and the warm-up done, feedback that grows a young player, a teammate conflict handled with accountability, and a mentor who hands the role on. It shares its court stations with the basketball programme, so a club that runs both trains the same coach.

**Unions:** AFSCME and SEIU parks-and-recreation staff who run youth programmes; taught against the youth-sport and child-safety guidelines, not a union apprenticeship

**Certification line:** The national youth-development guidelines for sport, the SafeSport code for keeping athletes safe from abuse, the CDC Heads Up concussion training, and trauma-informed practice
