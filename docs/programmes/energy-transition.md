## About this programme

The plant a utility is building now: solar, storage, charging and the substation that ties them together. Direct current behaves differently, and every station here is about that difference.

**Unions:** IBEW outside construction and utility locals

**Certification line:** NFPA 70E, NFPA 855 energy storage, NEC Article 690 photovoltaic systems and utility interconnection practice
