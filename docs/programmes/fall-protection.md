## About this programme

Anchor, connect, and the thing you are standing on. Four heights, four different reasons the system has to be right before the first step off the deck.

**Unions:** Ironworkers, Carpenters, CWA and NATE climbers

**Certification line:** OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M fall protection, Subpart L scaffolds, and ANSI Z359 personal fall-arrest systems
