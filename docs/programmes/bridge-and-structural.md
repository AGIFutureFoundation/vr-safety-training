## About this programme

The bridge as a workplace: cable and hanger inspection at height, lead-paint containment on a truss, deck joint replacement under traffic control, and the erection work the structural programme already teaches.

**Unions:** Ironworkers (IW), IUPAT bridge painters, IUOE operating engineers and LIUNA on the deck

**Certification line:** OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subparts M (fall protection), R (steel erection) and CC (cranes); 1926.62 lead in construction for bridge coatings; ANSI Z359; AWS D1.5 bridge welding; the owner's lane-closure and MUTCD traffic control plan

## Who it is for

The trades its stations are written for (from each station's `trade` field): Bridge painter — IUPAT; Ironworker / structural steel connector; Ironworker bridge maintenance crew with LIUNA traffic control — girder jacking and bearing replacement; Ironworker raising gang with IUOE crane operators — two-crane girder set; Ironworker with IUOE and LIUNA under a lane closure; Ironworker — bridge inspection, with the owner's inspector; Ironworker — structural bolting crew on a bridge girder field splice.
