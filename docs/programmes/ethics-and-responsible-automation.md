## About this programme

The entry point for deciding what a robot on a site is allowed to do, who answers for it, and what is said to the public when something goes wrong. Its stations are the governance and communication ones; the robot-control stations sit in the control entry point, so the two packs teach the same stop rule from two sides.

**Unions:** AFSCME and SEIU public-service staff, and the site supervisors who answer for an automated site; taught as a civic course, not a union apprenticeship

**Certification line:** The municipal ethics code, the conflict-of-interest rules that bind public officials, and the crisis-communication practice a public body is held to
