## About this programme

A ship comes alongside, is secured, is fuelled, is worked and leaves. Its stations follow that order, each with a different way to put oil or steel where it should not be.

**Unions:** ILWU longshore, MEBA and SIU marine engineers, IBT terminal drivers

**Certification line:** USCG 33 CFR 155/156 oil transfer, IMO Cargo Securing Manual practice, and OSHA 1917 marine terminals
