## About this programme

The kinds of work the U.S. EPA's San Francisco Bay Program awards of September 2026 pay for — trash capture devices, green stormwater infrastructure, tidal channels and sediment reuse, nutrient reduction at treatment plants, PCB source control and fish habitat — taught as the trade practice each job needs. The stations are generic and sited nowhere real; the Bay Program hub (WebXR/bayprogram/) links each named project to the stations that teach its work.

**Unions:** LIUNA laborers and LIUNA Local 261 shoreline crews, IUOE Local 3 operating engineers, AFSCME and SEIU 1021 public-agency operators and scientists

**Certification line:** Programme completion record; each station rehearses the practice its own bodies set — OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P for every cut, OSHA 29 CFR 1910.146 for any permit space, the MUTCD for work in the street and on the sidewalk, OSHA 1910.120 HAZWOPER inside any contaminated-sediment zone, and the Section 404/401 and BCDC permit conditions for work at the shoreline
