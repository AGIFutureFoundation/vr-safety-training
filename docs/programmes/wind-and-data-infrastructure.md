## About this programme

Eight jobs across a ridge wind farm, a data hall under construction and an ocean data pod: the climb proven on the ground, the nacelle locked and its yaw held, the blade platform's wind go/no-go, collector switching to a written order, a busway torqued and signed, one floor tile out and attended, a CRAH alarm answered in a live hall, and a sealed pod landed and isolated before its hatch opens. Each station runs with a declared ?fault= that changes the scene and one step's right answer, and no figure is ever stated — the manual, the order and the plans hold them.

**Unions:** IBEW and the IBEW/NECA JATC, IUOE, the Ironworkers and their IMPACT programme, the UA and SMART as training bodies

**Certification line:** Programme completion record; the credentials it rehearses are issued only by their own bodies — 29 CFR 1910.269 and 29 CFR 1910.147 for the turbine, the substation and the hall, NFPA 70E for every absence-of-voltage test, ANSI Z359 for the tower and the platform, NFPA 70 (NEC) and BICSI practice for the busway and the pathway; every height, wind, voltage, torque, load, depth and limit lives in the manufacturer's manual, the switching order, the lift plan or the dive plan
