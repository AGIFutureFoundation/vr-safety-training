## About this programme

The isolation habit, built four ways: a panel, a substation feeder, an overhead circuit and a live-load charger. Every station in this block ends with something proven dead before it is touched.

**Unions:** IBEW — International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers

**Certification line:** NFPA 70E work practices and OSHA 1910.147 control of hazardous energy, as taught in an IBEW/NECA JATC first-period block
