## About this programme

Ten stations on an indoor court that teach players the fundamentals and teach coaches to run a session nobody gets hurt in: the floor walked before anyone runs on it, contact introduced by size and stage, every knock to the head taken out of play, water and rest on the clock, and every heated moment, alarm and parent at the door handled calmly and in view.

**Unions:** AFSCME and SEIU parks-and-recreation staff where a public gym runs the league; the programme itself is taught against USA Basketball's youth guidelines rather than a union apprenticeship

**Certification line:** USA Basketball youth development guidelines for age-appropriate play, practice and rest; NFHS basketball rules and sports medicine guidance; CDC Heads Up concussion training; U.S. Center for SafeSport abuse-prevention policies; the American Red Cross first aid, CPR and AED course
