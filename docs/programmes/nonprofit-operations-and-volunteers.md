## About this programme

The entry point for running a non-profit well: a mission stated plainly, a board that meets in public, volunteers kept safe and trusted, grants asked for and accounted for. It shares its ethics and community stations with the other packs, so a learner who starts here meets the same standards again on the robot floor and the youth court.

**Unions:** SEIU and AFSCME community-service and public-sector staff, and the volunteers who run their programmes; taught as a civic course, not a union apprenticeship

**Certification line:** The federal 501(c)(3) rules for charitable organisations, the open-meeting and conflict-of-interest rules that apply to public boards, and the municipal ethics code
