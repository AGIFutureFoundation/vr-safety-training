Robot policy evaluation report

One card per trained policy from exports/robotics/ (node tools/rb_export.mjs): the scenario, the data it learned from, the seeds it was judged on, its success against a random floor and the scripted expert, the safe-practice invariants it broke, and where it sits in the ENTERPRISE-3 lineage with the audit chain's head. Written by node tools/rb_report.mjs; a static page with no script.

Simulated robots only. Every policy here was trained on synthetic demonstrations (a scripted expert with lapses and action noise, labelled synthetic; no learner episode) and evaluated on the simulated scenario. No hardware run has happened and none is claimed; docs/bench-protocol.md describes how one would be scored.

Method, named plainly: behaviour cloning with a k-nearest-neighbour classifier over hand-written features (WebXR/shared/col-learn.js). It copies the action the most similar demonstrated moments took; it does not plan and does not generalise beyond its demonstrations. Success figures come from docs/evals/colearn.json (tools/col_eval.mjs); invariant violations are counted live by the exporter over the same seeds. The audit chain is FNV-1a hashed, a local tamper-evidence aid and not cryptographic proof. Registry head 6badf45ae6cf5ea0; registry built in memory, no device store read or written.
